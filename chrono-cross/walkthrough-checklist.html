New Game
Chrono Cross uses a unique stat-growth system instead of traditional experience points. Regular battles grant small stat increases, but only up to a cap; once battles stop giving gains, you've hit it. Larger, permanent stat boosts come only from defeating specific "boss" fights, each of which awards a numbered level-up star (tracked throughout this guide). All active party members must survive a boss fight to get its full stat bonus. Characters sitting outside the active party miss out on stat gains entirely until the Tele-Porter key item allows swapping them in.

RECRUITMENT CHOICE: Invite Kid?
Accepting Kid permanently forfeits recruiting Leena later; refusing three times keeps Kid out for now and lets Leena join automatically instead. Only one of the two can be recruited in a single normal playthrough.

09) Guldove [Another World]
STORY CHOICE: Try to save Kid?
Choosing "Find a way to save her" recruits Korcha, Mel, and Razzly, and keeps Kid's absence from the party relatively short. Choosing "...I don't know" or "...There's nothing we can do" recruits Doc, Macha, and Glenn instead, and delays Kid rejoining the party until very late in the game.
Recommended: Find a way to save her. Future steps in this guide assume Kid was saved.

13) Fort Dragonia [Another World]
Stock up on Iceberg, HolyLight, Inferno, and Volcano traps before entering; none of these will be purchasable again for quite a while after this dungeon, and they're all useful against upcoming bosses.
A Dragoon guards the entrance and can be fought if the Water Dragon Isle detour (and its FrogPrince summon) was skipped; defeating it drops the FrogPrince and awards the 15th level-up star. It doesn't appear on New Game+.
The main objective across this floor is deactivating a series of colored obelisks (Red, Yellow, Green, Blue, and White), each behind its own puzzle room; a final Black destination is unlocked by defeating its guardian boss directly rather than a separate deactivation step.
Fort Dragonia: Red Room
Whatever direction the statue faces represents north; from there, pick the direction that would extend over the correct hole (per the room's own diagram: if the statue faces north, choose east; if south, choose west, and so on). Only the northern hole leads to enemy encounters (Cybots); there are infinite retries if the wrong hole is chosen.
The four holes also contain a Volcano Trap, Flame Charm, MagmaBurst, and Fire Pillar.
Once the pillar is found, the pattern in the branch room lights up to confirm it. Head to the Yellow Room next.
Fort Dragonia: Yellow Room
How the maze itself moves depends on current party formation. The exact starting order (and so the exact fix needed) also depends on formation; use Select outside to swap leaders, and the glowing blue pad there to shift the order further, until the Head-Body-Tail arrangement is reached. Inspect the pedestal again afterward to reconfigure the maze.
Earth Charm: from a chest reachable in the Body-Tail-Head order.
ElectroBolt: from a chest reachable via stairs, lowered by a blue switch inside the maze, in the Tail-Head-Body order.
Fort Dragonia: Green Room
Antidote: an out-of-the-way loop to the left from there.
Tornado Trap: in a south cul-de-sac after heading east from the start.
AeroBlaster: down the lower fork where the northern walkway splits (the upper fork leads to the pillar and boss).
Trigger the blue switch along the way to lower a stairway shortcut back. Save with any loot collected before continuing on.
BOSS: Taurusoid
Innate: Green   HP: 1600   Spoils: @Carapace, Iron Vest
It heals itself with HealAll if hit by yellow elements, so stick to normal attacks and techs; a Weaken helps since it otherwise deals around 70 damage per hit. All of its specials are single-target and it's fairly slow, making it easy to keep the field effect clear of green. After its first three turns, it randomly settles into one of two attack routines, one manageable and one considerably more hectic with frequent double-tech chains; try to avoid letting the field effect go fully green, which seems to encourage the harder routine. Tornado Trap and Sonja Trap are both stealable; separately, despite its name, Tornado itself is never actually cast as an attack in practice. 1200G awarded, plus a 16th level-up star.
Fort Dragonia: Blue Room
Sea Charm: in the leftmost room.
The eastmost room leads to a Dragoon enemy fight, which can be skipped if preferred. On New Game+, the Dragoon and Sea Charm rooms are swapped.
BOSS: GiantGloop
Innate: Blue   HP: 840   Spoils: AquaBall, IceBlast
It uses a dangerous trappable Iceberg attack once weakened, which counters offensive red elements below 12.5% HP; re-trap it each time it's used, or hold off on red elements against it while weakened. Capturing Iceberg for later use requires a Lvl 6 element slot; Luccia, Greco, or Mojo all have one by 16 stars if needed, and it can be trapped, fled from, and kept, letting the fight be retried for more copies. Antiviral Cap is a separate stealable item. Otherwise, red elements like MagmaBurst, RedPin, FirePillar, or Inferno work well here, particularly from a red-innate character with high Magic. 800G awarded, plus a 17th level-up star.
Equip any captured Iceberg on a blue-innate character before the next fight; it can subtract as much as 2 full elemental levels from the target. Bring a HolyLight trap too.
BOSS: SunOfAGun
Innate: White   HP: 365   Spoils: @Iron
Its defense is solid despite modest HP, and its attack pattern shifts based on a facial-expression cycle (ModeChange). Its "blank" mode carries a trappable HolyLight, useful against black-innates and instant-kill on undead; it can be trapped, then fled and re-fought to stock up on multiple copies, which pays off against the black-innate boss coming up next. The "angry" mode unleashes three attacks in a row, so heal up if it appears; Imbecile is worth applying here too, since its damage output rises as the field effect turns white. @Screw is stealable. Black elements or strong physical damage work best overall. 840G awarded, plus an 18th level-up star.
The elevator also descends to some broken incubators; take it up instead for the main path. If Inferno or Volcano traps haven't been bought at Marbule yet, do so before heading up.
MeteorShower and Angel Charm: past a small doorway nearby.
Daemon Charm: guarded by a single Acacia SGT, just before the black obelisk's door.
BOSS: Bunyip (Phase 1 → 2)
Innate: Red   HP: 400   Spoils: n/a
Innate: Black   HP: 1000   Spoils: Gravitonne, HellBound
This two-phase fight is a great source of Inferno and Volcano elements, but only during the red-innate first phase; trap them before it transitions, since they can't be recovered afterward, and the fight can be fled and retried to farm extra copies before finishing it off. The second (black) phase uses a trappable FreeFall; BlackHole Trap is also stealable. MeteorShower, HolyLight, and Luminaire cut through its HP quickly, with periodic HealAlls to offset DevilThunda. Keep the field effect away from all-black. 1300G awarded, plus a 19th level-up star.
This is the last opportunity for a long while to buy Inferno, Deluge, ThundaStorm, HolyLight, FreeFall, or Carnivore traps; stock up now if any are wanted.
BOSS: Gen. Viper
Innate: Yellow   HP: 820   Spoils: Dragoon Gauntlet, Mythril Mail
A mild fight overall, mostly threatening to green characters and not severely even then. Stamina Ring is stealable. A good opportunity to use any trapped Volcano, Inferno, or FreeFall elements, since his attack pattern allows plenty of room to heal. No gold is awarded, but a 20th level-up star is.
BOSS: Lynx
Innate: Black   HP: 1000   Steal: Pendragon Sigil B, Sky Djinn Ring
Lynx can borrow one of Serge's own elements for the fight, making it temporarily unusable on Serge's own grid (though he won't take anything critical). His only black attack, GlideHook, can deal 100+ damage and occasionally strikes three or four times in one turn, hitting Serge especially hard. Keep Serge above 150 HP and everyone else above 100 at all times; bombard him with trapped elements while using HiRes/BatEye for support. 666G awarded, plus a 21st level-up star.
An unwinnable story battle follows immediately afterward; there's nothing to do but let it play out. This closes out Fort Dragonia.
