Lahan Village
The Mermaid Tear and RPS Badge are unique trade items with no second chance. The RPS Badge is only available until you meet Citan at his mountaintop house.
DIALOGUE CHOICE: Sulky man in the bar
"Drinking won't help matters"
Traded much later at the Snowfield Hideout, after Emeralda becomes an adult, for the Mermaid Ring (Emeralda only).
One of three badges. Trade all three to the old Chu-Chu in Shevat for a Charger A and a Power Ring S.
Mountain Path
Give it to Chu-Chu during Road to Nisan for an Etherveiler.
Citan's House
Timing matters: take it any sooner and the egg gets cooked; any later and it disappears. The mother bird deals 1 damage every few seconds until you return to Citan's House.
Trade it to Midori in the final chapter (First and Last) for a HerculesRing, which gives 50 more EXP during battle.
Save at the save point at the mountain base before continuing.
Lahan Village (Fallen Shadows)
Start learning Deathblows now. Raijin (Triangle, X) is Fei's first and easiest; Senretsu (Triangle, Triangle, X) may be close if you have been training. Check learning progress under Status on the main menu.
